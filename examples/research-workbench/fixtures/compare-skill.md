# Compare supplied manuals

1. Read the relevant pages of both manuals before comparing a feature.
2. Keep each observation attached to its document and source receipt.
3. Compare one concrete question at a time: export format, saving, or offline use.
4. Preserve conditions such as plan tier and page scope in the comparison.
5. If a page is silent, report that gap. Silence alone does not establish that a feature is absent.
6. Finish with the supported comparison and a short list of unresolved questions.
